Adapted from <https://smittenkitchen.com/2008/12/sugar-and-spice-candied-nuts/>,
<https://smittenkitchen.com/2016/12/union-square-cafes-bar-nuts/>,
and <https://www.seriouseats.com/how-to-make-spiced-nuts-holiday-entertaining-hors-dourves>.

In a large bowl, whisk until frothy but not stiff:

- 1 egg white
- 1 T water

Stir in nuts to coat:

- 16 oz walnuts or other nuts
  (maybe 8-12 oz for more assertive flavors?)

Drain excess egg wash.
Toss nuts with one of the spice mixes below.
Bake on a parchment-lined sheet pan at 300 F for 30 minutes, stirring every 5-10 minutes.

### Spicy rosemary mix (Union Square Cafe)

- 1/2 c granulated sugar
- 1/2 c light brown sugar
- 1.5 tsp table salt
- 3/4 tsp chipotle powder, or cayenne
- 1.5 T dried rosemary, ground to powder
