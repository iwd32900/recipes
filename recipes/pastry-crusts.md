These two recipes feature similar ingredients and techniques,
but the different flour/fat/water ratios and size of butter pieces leads to different textures.

# Pie crust

Makes 1 crust; double recipe for a 2-crust pie.
Adapted from [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/easy-pie-dough-recipe).

Into a large bowl, coarsely grate
- 113 g butter or plant butter (1 stick)

Place bowl in freezer for 5 minutes.

Add
- 90 g soft all-purpose flour

Whisk vigorously until thoroughly incorporated and butter-flour paste is sandy, or until clumps begin to form.

Sprinkle over top and whisk just to mix
- 50 g soft all-purpose flour
- 1 T sugar (for browning)
- 1/2 t table salt

Using a spatula, gradually incorporate
- 35 g of ice-cold water

Use the spatula to fold and press the dough down, until it comes together in a cohesive mass.
The folding action helps form flaky layers.

Generously flour the dough.
Roll dough into a circle somewhat larger than the pie plate.
Flip the dough periodically and add plenty of flour to prevent sticking.
Fit into pie plate and flute edges if making bottom crust.
Wrap in plastic and refrigerate at least 2 hours to allow gluten to fully relax, so that crust doesn't shrink in oven.

**Blind baking**:  20 minutes with pie weights, followed by 15 minutes without, at 375 F.

# Rough puff pastry

Makes 10 oz of pastry; double if needed.
Adapted from [Gordon Ramsay / BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/rough-puff-pastry).
[Stella Parks](https://www.seriouseats.com/old-fashioned-flaky-pie-dough-recipe) uses a similar recipe for her pies.

Into a large bowl, coarsely grate
- 113 g butter or plant butter (1 stick)

Place bowl in freezer for 5 minutes.

Add
- 113 g King Arthur all-purpose flour
- 1/2 t table salt

Using a spatula, stir just to combine.
Gradually incorporate
- 50 g of ice-cold water

Use the spatula to fold and press the dough down, until it comes together in a cohesive mass.
The folding action helps form flaky layers.

Refrigerate 20+ minutes.
Generously flour the dough.
Roll dough out into a long rectangle, and fold into thirds, brushing off excess flour from interior surfaces.
Turn 90 degrees and repeat rolling and folding.
Wrap dough and refrigerate another 20+ minutes.

Roll out dough and use as desired.
Trim edges with sharp knife for best rise.
