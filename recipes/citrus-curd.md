# Citrus Curd

Combine in a clean, 8 oz canning jar:

- 2 oz eggs (about 1 whole + 1 yolk, or about 3 yolks)
- 2 oz lemon or key lime juice, fresh if possible
- 2 oz sugar
- 2 T butter, if desired
- citrus zest, if desired
- pinch of salt

Cap tightly, and shake hard to combine.
Loosen cap slightly and cook in a water bath at 180 F for 1 hour.
Re-tighten cap, and shake hard to emulsify.
Store in refrigerator.

The "equal parts by weight" recipe comes from Stella Parks.
