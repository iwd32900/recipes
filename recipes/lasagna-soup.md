# Lasagna Soup

Based on several recipes from *Trader Joe's One-Stop Meals* by Brittney Hanks.

In a **large** pot, brown:

- 1 lb. Italian sausage
- 1 onion, diced
- olive oil

Stir in:

- garlic (before liquids)
- 24 oz jar marinara sauce
- 64 oz chicken broth (or water + 3 T Better than Bullion)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 16 oz dried pasta
- 10 oz fresh spinach (near the end of cooking)

Simmer until pasta is tender.  Serve with:

- ricotta cheese
- grated Parmesean
